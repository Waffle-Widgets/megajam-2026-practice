# Jam rules check

Source of truth: `docs/RULES.md` (summary) and the official PDF it points to (the PDF wins). Re-read `docs/RULES.md`
when unsure; don't rely on memory.

## Ask these questions
1. **Timing (§7h).** Before Nov 12, only AI tools, agent configs (`CLAUDE.md`, skills), MCP settings, prompt libraries
   and scripts may be prepared. Gameplay, levels and art must be made Nov 12-15. Epic may check the Git history.
   Practice work in this repo never goes into the jam repo.
2. **Theme (§7g).** Does it use the theme, and does the game go well beyond the Third Person template (§7d, §7e)?
3. **Content (§8).** Suitable for ages 13+; English only, including in-game text, audio and the video (§7i).
4. **IP.** No trademarks, logos or real brands (except the Unreal Engine logo), no real people's likeness, and no
   recreating another game's names, characters, art, music or levels. Real car models: no badges or brand names.
5. **Assets (§15c).** Sourced assets (Fab, sound libraries) are allowed but not scored. Ours (Blender, code, `make_sfx.py`)
   and AI-generated ones are scored, so prefer those. Anything not ours goes in `CREDITS.md` with source and licence.
6. **Scope.** One level, one core mechanic, 3-5 minutes of play. Push back on multiplayer, story/dialogue systems,
   inventories, complex enemy AI or open worlds, and offer a simpler version.
7. **Team and submission (§2, §17).** At most 5 members, all 18+ with an Epic account, every one listed on the entry
   (name + Epic email; keep `docs/TEAM.md` current). A human submits, not an agent: the Team Owner.

## How to answer
Say which rule a request touches, quote the section number, and give a version that's allowed. Don't silently refuse
or silently go ahead.
